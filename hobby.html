Running is one of my favorite ways to stay active, clear my mind, and challenge myself.

I enjoy working toward race goals and improving my endurance. Each run is an opportunity to practice consistency and see progress over time.

Why I run

Progress comes from showing up.

A goal to work toward

Training for a race gives me something concrete to focus on. I enjoy building endurance and finding a pace that I can improve with practice.

Balance beyond the screen

Running adds balance to the time I spend working and studying. I also enjoy boxing, lifting weights, and walking my Doberman.

For me, running and programming both reward patience, practice, and steady effort.
